Job offers from Kraków BIP and job boards

A daily pipeline that collects job offers from the Public Information Bulletin of the City of Kraków (over 500 municipal units) and from job boards, keeps their availability history (first seen, active, expired) and matches them to a career profile. The reports below are static snapshots of real runs.

Snapshots from 22 October 2026, in Polish. The offers come from public job adverts; most of them have expired since. Filters, sorting and the district map work in the browser, without a server.
